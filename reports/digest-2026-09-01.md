## Ideas to improve the app

### 1. Make the daily quality check actually catch outages like this one

You had a daily quality snapshot running right through this period (files exist
for 25, 26, 27 and 28 August), and it did not flag that the app was completely
broken on the evening of 28 August. Then it stopped producing files altogether
on 29 August.

Two things worth checking: whether that job genuinely calls the live Anthropic
API (if it uses saved test data, it will never notice a broken API call), and
why it has produced nothing for three days. A check that fails loudly when a
real end-to-end run errors would have told you within hours — instead of five
users finding out for you.

*Grounded in: 5 failures over 13 hours went undetected; snapshot files stop at 2026-08-28.*

### 2. Show users a plain-English error instead of the raw Python one

Your frontend prints the server's raw error text straight to the screen. This
week that meant two prospective users read a Python type error. One of them
tried three times before giving up, and neither returned.

A friendly fallback — something like *"Something went wrong on our end. We've
been notified — please try again in a few minutes."* — with the technical detail
still logged to Supabase for you, would cost you nothing in diagnostics and
would not scare off the handful of users you have. Given your traffic is roughly
two real users a week right now, losing both to an error screen is expensive.

*Grounded in: 5 identical raw errors shown to users; 3 retries from one person; no return visits.*

### 3. Look into why only about half the researched grants reach the final list

Your app deep-researches 10 candidate grants in Phase 3 (`SHORTLIST_SIZE = 10`
in `analyzer.py:167`), but the finished reports contain far fewer: an average of
4.75 over the last month, and only 3 this week. So roughly half the grants you
spend research time and money on get dropped before the user sees them.

That might be entirely correct — the rubric may be filtering out genuinely poor
fits, which is the app doing its job. But it's worth confirming, because if
they're being dropped for a fixable reason (formatting, output length limits,
over-strict scoring) you're paying for research the user never benefits from.
A user who gets 3 results may feel the tool didn't find much.

Side note: your `CLAUDE.md` says to keep `SHORTLIST_SIZE` at 8 because the
scoring output is capped at 8192 tokens, but the code now has it at 10. Worth
reconciling — if 10 candidates' worth of scoring doesn't fit in the token cap,
that could itself be why results are getting truncated.

*Grounded in: 10 candidates researched vs 4.75 average delivered across 8 completed runs.*
