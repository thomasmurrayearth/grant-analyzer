### The drop-off problem — already fixed, on 30 July

Seven of the 24 runs (**29%**) never reached a result: 5 stopped at
`profile_ready` (the user got their company profile, then never continued into
the grant search) and 2 stopped at `started`.

**All seven happened before 30 July.** That's the date you shipped server-side
auto-continue (commit `57702ce`), which starts Phase 2+3 on a 15-second server
timer whether or not the browser stays open. Splitting the history at that date:

| Period | Runs | Completed | Stalled |
|---|---|---|---|
| Before 30 July | 15 | 8 | **7 (47%)** |
| Since 30 July | 9 | **9 (100%)** | 0 |

The leak is closed. The 29% figure is a historical average that mixes in the
pre-fix era, not a live problem.

**One residual risk worth knowing about:** the auto-continue timer lives in the
app's memory (`main.py:399-424`), and there's no recovery on startup — the
startup handler only arms the benchmark loop (`main.py:147`). If Railway restarts
during the 15-second window, that run is orphaned and nothing resumes it. The
window is narrow and it hasn't happened in 9 runs, so this is a "know about it"
rather than a "fix it now".

## Ideas to improve the app

### 1. Email your two known users this week — you have no other way to learn why usage stopped

You have exactly two named users and both are recent and engaged: Georgie Simms
at F2F Systems (whose company has run the tool 7 times, more than anyone) and
Jack Norton at Deep Science Ventures (who ran two analyses back-to-back on 11
August). Usage has now been silent for 10 days and you have zero feedback
submissions ever, so the database cannot tell you why. A short "you used this a
few weeks ago — was the shortlist any use?" email to two people is the cheapest
piece of research available to you, and it is grounded in the fact that these are
literally your only contactable users.

### 2. Your live leak is now the landing page, not the pipeline

The old drop-off at "profile ready" is fixed (see above), so the funnel's weak
point has moved to the very top. Across the ~4 weeks the events table has been
recording, **30 landing views produced only 10 started analyses** — and this
week it was 3 views and zero starts. Two strangers arrived, looked, and left
without clicking anything.

That is where the attention should go. You cannot diagnose it from the database
alone, because you log a landing view but nothing in between — no scroll depth,
no "clicked into the form then stopped". One extra event fired when someone
focuses the URL box would tell you whether people are bouncing off the pitch or
stalling at the form, and that is the difference between rewriting the homepage
and simplifying the input.

### 3. Drop or rethink the "priority geographies" box

In 24 runs across 9 different companies, not one person has ever typed anything
into this field. It is dead weight on a form where every extra box is a reason to
bounce — and this matters more than usual for you, because your funnel shows
people landing and not starting. Either remove it, or replace the free-text box
with two or three clickable buttons (UK / EU / Global), which people will tap
when they won't type. If geography actually improves the shortlist quality, the
better move is to infer it from the company profile you already build in Phase 1
rather than asking the user at all.
