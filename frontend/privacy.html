2. What personal data we collect and why

When you use this tool, we record the following information about each analysis session:

Data Why we collect it Legal basis (UK/EU GDPR)
Company website URL you enter To deliver the analysis; to understand what types of companies use this tool Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f))
Company description text (first 500 characters of any pasted text) To understand what types of companies use this tool and improve results quality Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f))
Search preferences (geography, grant types) To tailor results; to understand user needs Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f))
AI-generated company profile To record analysis outputs and improve the AI pipeline Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f))
Grant analysis results To record outputs and improve the scoring methodology Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f))
IP address To detect and prevent abuse; to understand the geographic spread of users Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f))
Date and time of analysis To understand usage patterns Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f))

What we do NOT collect:

  • Your name or email address (unless you contact us directly)
  • Payment or financial information
  • Information from or about children
  • Cookies or persistent browser tracking identifiers

Legitimate interest balancing test: We have assessed that our legitimate interest in improving the quality of this tool does not override users' rights and freedoms, because: the data is predominantly business-related (company URLs, grant searches) rather than personal; we store only a brief snippet of any pasted text; we do not use the data for marketing or share it with advertisers; and users can request deletion at any time (see section 6).

3. Who we share your data with

We use the following third-party services to operate this tool. Each acts as a data processor on our behalf and is contractually required to handle data securely:

Supabase Inc.

supabase.com — database storage

Stores the analytics data described above on servers located in the United States. For transfers from the UK and EU, we rely on Supabase's Data Processing Agreement and Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs). See supabase.com/privacy.

Anthropic PBC

anthropic.com — AI processing

Receives the company information you provide in order to generate the grant analysis. Processing takes place on Anthropic's servers in the United States. Anthropic's API usage terms apply and they do not use API inputs to train their models by default. See anthropic.com/privacy.

DuckDuckGo

duckduckgo.com — web search

The AI generates web search queries based on your company inputs to research grant opportunities. We do not send your raw input text directly to DuckDuckGo — only AI-generated search terms. See duckduckgo.com/privacy.

We do not sell your personal data to any third party, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

4. International data transfers

Both Supabase and Anthropic store and process data in the United States. The US does not have an adequacy decision from the UK or EU in all cases. Where required, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) as the legal mechanism for these transfers, as incorporated into each provider's Data Processing Agreement. You can request a copy of the relevant safeguards by emailing us.

5. How long we keep your data

We retain analysis records for 12 months from the date of the analysis. After this period, records are permanently deleted. You may request earlier deletion at any time (see section 6).

6. Your rights

If you are in the UK or EU (UK GDPR / EU GDPR Article 15–22):

Access Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
Rectification Ask us to correct inaccurate data.
Erasure Ask us to delete your data ("right to be forgotten").
Restriction Ask us to limit how we use your data.
Portability Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
Object Object to processing based on legitimate interests. We will cease processing unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds.

If you are in California, USA (CCPA / CPRA):

  • Right to know what personal information we collect
  • Right to request deletion of your personal information
  • Right to opt out of the sale of personal information — we do not sell personal information
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights

How to exercise your rights

Email thomasmurraynz@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Request". Please include the approximate date of your analysis and the company URL you entered so we can locate your record. We will respond within 30 days (UK/EU) or 45 days (California).

Because we do not collect names or emails during analysis, we may need to use your IP address or analysis timestamp to locate your record.

7. Right to complain to a supervisory authority

If you believe we have not handled your data lawfully, you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority:

  • United Kingdom: Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) — ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint
  • European Union: Your national data protection authority (a full list is at edpb.europa.eu)
  • California, USA: California Privacy Protection Agency (CPPA) — cppa.ca.gov

We would, however, appreciate the opportunity to address your concern directly before you contact a regulator.

8. Automated decision-making

Grant analysis results are produced entirely by automated AI processing. These results do not produce legal effects or otherwise significantly affect you — they are informational suggestions to help you research grant opportunities. You remain free to disregard any output and no decisions about you are made based on this processing.

9. Cookies and tracking

This tool does not use cookies, tracking pixels, analytics scripts, or any other persistent browser tracking technology.

10. Children

This service is not directed at, and is not intended for use by, anyone under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has submitted data through this tool, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.
